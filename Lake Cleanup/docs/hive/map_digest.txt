AREA: The pump as a world object on the island: the template a Hive node has to mirror (Lake Cleanup/scripts/pump.gd, and every place in lake.gd, player.gd, dog.gd, flora.gd, wildlife.gd, puddles.gd, weathe
SUMMARY: The pump (`class_name Pump extends Node2D`, scripts/pump.gd, 131 lines) is a front-on picture on the island's lawn. Its position is a static, `Pump.tile`, and walkers query it through static `covers()`/`hides()` instead of being handed a reference. `Lake._ready` builds it on CRATE_LAYER 8 and sets the static before `_grow_nature()` and `_start_weather()`, whose `_ready`s sow flora, find shore spots and lay puddles using that static. It is wired into 12 functions in 6 scripts:
- lake.gd: `_walker_layer`, `_weather_roofs`, `_weather_statics`, `_draw_shed_lamp`, the desk and pad interact chains, `_at_pump`
- player.gd: `_slide`, `_can_stand`
- dog.gd: `_bumped`, `_may_stand`
- flora.gd: `_sow`
- wildlife.gd: `_add_shore`, `_on_sand`
- puddles.gd: `may_lie`

Tests: `test_lake._stage_wash` (lines 7902-7928) and the flora check at 6446. Probe: `tools/shot_pump.gd`.

A Hive at about tile (43.3, 47.3), which is `Iso.shed_centre()` (45.320, 45.320) + (-2.02, +1.98), mirrors all of these. That spot is 128 world px left of the hut and level with its middle. It is 2.36 tiles from the door stand (45.55, 46.60) and 4.8 tiles from the pump (47.42, 44.82), so its E range cannot overlap either.

The pump has one scalar square footprint, centres its picture horizontally on its foot, bakes one image, and keys its shadow cache on the box alone. None of that holds for a hive+shelf sprite with several states. Some of the pump's wiring also has latent bugs that should not be copied: the wash room leaves the angler free to walk, U/pad-Y opens the shop over it, `roof()` copies an Image every frame, and the hut slide measures from the wrong centre. There are also three stale comment/doc contradictions to name before building on them.

-- PITFALLS
! **The static must be set before `_grow_nature()` / `_start_weather()`** (lake.gd:1216-1217). Flora._ready → `_sow`, Wildlife._ready → `_find_shore` and Puddles._ready → `_lay` each compute their spots exactly once. Setting `Hive.tile` later leaves flowers, frogs' shore spots and puddles under the hive for the whole session.
! **A new `class_name Hive` must reach Godot's global class cache** (a run with `<exe> --path . --headless --import`, or an editor rescan). Until then every script that says `Hive.covers` (player.gd, dog.gd, flora.gd, wildlife.gd, puddles.gd, lake.gd) fails to parse in a headless test_lake run, and parse errors only show in `--log-file`. The same applies to a new `assets/hive.png` (reimport).
! **The pump's footprint is one scalar square (`FOOT_HALF` on both axes)**. A hive with a shelf running up-left along -tx is a rectangle, or hive and shelf are two boxes. With the pump's square:
- walkers walk through the shelf posts;
- `_walker_layer`'s 'north of both near faces' rule misdraws a walker standing just in front of the shelf, putting it behind the whole sprite.

Use Vector2 half-extents centred on the combined base, consistently in `covers`, `_walker_layer`, `_bumped`, `_slide` and the tests.
! **`Pump._box`, `hides`, `drawn_wide` and `_walker_layer`'s `abs(at.x - pos.x) < drawn_wide*0.5` all assume the picture is horizontally centred on the foot.** The mockup's hive stand sits at x = 30 of 44, so either anchor the node at the picture's centre and keep the tile at the base, or add an x anchor to the JSON contract and use a drawn Rect2 everywhere.
! **Shade.sweep and Skirt.hem treat anything whose lowest pixel is well above the picture's deepest row as an overhang.** Sweep uses its `ground` band; hem uses `HEM_BAND` = 0.3 of the height. The pump passes its placement `_ground` (0.111) as the sweep band too. The shelf's left post feet (about row 19-20 of about 40, against the stand at about 35) would get **no grass and no shadow**. Decouple the sweep band from the placement ground, the way the hut uses 0.2382, or draw the shelf as its own node with its own foot.
! **`Shade.Cast.lay` caches on the key `"box|ground"` only, not on the image** (shade.gd:197-208). When the hive changes state or gains jars with the same box, the shadow is NOT re-laid and keeps the old silhouette. Recreate the Cast, or fold state and jar count into what `lay` sees, on every change.
! **`Pump.roof()`, `Yard.roof()` and the hut's roof all call `Art.image(path)`, and `_weather_roofs()` runs every Weather frame whether or not it is raining** (weather.gd:235). `Texture2D.get_image()` hands back a fresh Image each call, so:
- three image copies are made per frame;
- `Weather._tops_cache` (keyed by `get_instance_id()`) never hits, and grows by one entry per roof landing.

`Pump._draw` also calls `Art.image(ART)` every frame just to pass it to `lay`, which usually returns early. Cache the Image once, as `Dropoff._box_image` does (dropoff.gd:453-458), and return the same instance.
! **The pump's room plumbing has bugs that must not be copied into the hive room:**
- `_hold_the_angler` (lake.gd:3582-3593) omits `_wash_open`, and `_set_wash` never calls it. With the wash room up, WASD walks the angler behind the room, and on the pad the left stick both aims the nozzle (`WashRoom._pad_aim` reads `walk_*`, wash_room.gd:410) and walks him.
- The `open_upgrades` guards (desk lake.gd:2166; pad 2330, checked before `busy`) don't exclude `_wash_open`, so U or pad Y opens the shop over the room.

Add `_hive_open` to both.
! **The weather roof order matters**: test_lake:6138 reads `(_roofs)[0]` as the hut. Append the hive after `_yard`/`_pump`, never before the shed.
! **`Puddles.draw_reflections` and `Weather._roof_top` stretch the WHOLE texture or image over `rect`.** Handing them a sheet with several hive states plus a region rect draws the whole sheet upside down in the puddle and gives wrong roof heights. `roof()` must return a cropped, cached per-state Image and Texture.
! **`Pump.hides` prefilters with `Pump.covers(at, 2.5)`**, sized for a 54 px-tall pump. The hive plus shelf is about 80 px tall; 2.5 tiles on each axis reaches only about 80-90 px up-screen. Too small a prefilter silently lets flowers grow 'painted onto' the hive's roof or shelf, and test_lake's flora check would pass vacuously if it copied the same radius.
! **The bee-host count has a different meaning**: `Flora.bee_count()` is lake-wide, capped at `BEES_MOST` 90, and holds 1-2 entries per host. `_find_bees` calls anything with `island_fraction < 1.5` 'near', which reaches the shore water. Gating the swarm on 'grown bee-host flowers on the island' needs a new accessor using `island_fraction < 1.0`. Excluding flowers around the hive (`covers`/`hides` in `_sow`) also lowers the island host count slightly.
! **`_moment_owed` is a single Vector2**, and `_start_owed_moment` hard-codes `Text.WILDLIFE_BACK` and sets `_wildlife_seen`. A swarm moment owed at the same time as the first-wildlife moment overwrites it, or is shown with the wrong words, unless the moment is generalised.
! **The lamp**:
- `_pump.lit` is written inside the `_island` node's draw callback (`_draw_shed_lamp`), so it can land a frame late; set `_hive.lit` from `_process` instead.
- The pump draws its lamp at `box.position.y - 10`. A hive that also draws a 'ready' mark over its roof in the full state will stack the two in one spot.
! **Z-order assumption**: `BEHIND_CRATE` (7) is above `SHED_LAYER` (6). Putting the hive on `CRATE_LAYER` (8) with walkers behind it at 7 is correct only while the hive's drawn span does not overlap the hut's picture. At (43.3, 47.3) the gap is about 17-26 px (hive right edge about -100 against the hut's left edge at -73.5). Moving `HIVE_AT` right, or widening the sprite, makes walkers behind the hive draw over the hut.
! **The door**: the door stand is (45.55, 46.60), 2.36 tiles from the hive. HIVE_RANGE 1.5 plus DOOR_RANGE 0.6 = 2.1, so there is no overlap now; a larger range or a nudged `HIVE_AT` makes E ambiguous by the door.

**test_lake's hut dog trial** detours via the corner at about (43.70, 46.62), roughly 0.25 tiles from a 0.44-half-extent hive box. A bigger footprint could make that trial hug or stall.
! **Puddles are seeded** (seed 90125, 600 tries, COUNT 3, APART 4.5). Adding a hive clause to `may_lie` can change which spots are chosen. Re-read the rain stage's puddle checks, and the `hides` / roof-landing expectation, after adding it.
! **Probe safety**: a probe must use its own `save_path`, `autoload_save = false`, and a lake under its own Node, never the root. It must quit on a wall clock. Read its log, not only its exit code. Headless compiles no shaders and draws nothing, so hive visuals and any hive shader are only proved by the desktop probe.
! **Stale comments in the template** (pump near `SHOP_RANGE`; 'asked before `_at_shed`, which it stands inside'; the wash close 'returns to the lake'; the settings board muffled; the hut slide from `ISLAND_CENTRE`) are contradictions under the vigilance rule. Name them to Richard rather than copying them.

AREA: The wash room as the template for the hive room: scripts/wash_room.gd (whole), its WashBackdrop/WashStand wiring, and every lake.gd hook that opens, closes, hides, gates and sounds it
SUMMARY: The wash room is a full-screen `Control` built lazily on the HUD `CanvasLayer`. The HUD sets no `layer` in main.tscn, so it is on layer 1. The room is appended after the gear and the settings board, so it covers both. Its first child is a `WashBackdrop` (a standalone class the hive room can reuse as is). Over that come a `WashStand` (the reveal mask, tied to catalogue pieces), a tray and a `CloseButton`.

The lake owns the room. `_set_wash` builds it, lends it callables and hides `_skin` and the coins. The room is in `_panelled()` and in `pad_cursor_wanted()`, and `_push_rooms` muffles the music. It is not in `_hold_the_angler()`, it is not paused and it is not `Sfx.indoors`, so the lake runs on behind it. The player closing it goes to the shed (`_wash_to_shed`); a forced close (`_enter_menu`) calls `_set_wash(false)` and goes to the lake.

The same template carries real gaps a hive room would copy:
- The angler is not held: walk keys and the left stick walk him behind the room.
- The net keeps reeling, and the find card (layer 19) and the pigeon head (layer 18) draw over the room.
- Keyboard U, pad Y and pad Start open the shop or settings underneath it.
- The wildlife moment is one slot with fixed text and a fixed flag.

AREA: Lake Cleanup/scripts/wash_stand.gd (plus the parts of wash_room.gd, pad.gd, lake.gd, test_lake.gd and the probes that drive it), read to see what an 'uncap' minigame in the hive room can reuse
SUMMARY: WashStand (class_name WashStand, extends Control, 1402 lines) is one self-contained Control that does five things:

1. **Reveal mask.** Two parallel arrays over the restored find, `_solid` (PackedByteArray) and `_grime` (PackedFloat32Array). The grid is FINE (2) cells per painted pixel each way. A cols x rows RGBA8 Image, `_coat`, holds one pixel per cell and is drawn scaled, nearest-filtered, over the art. It is repainted one pixel at a time, and only when a cell crosses a step line. It is pushed to the GPU once a frame (`_coat_tex.update`).
2. **Jet.** Radial brush `_wash_at`, with trickles, spray flecks and bubbles simulated by hand.
3. **Finish machine.** WASHING -> RINSING -> SHINING -> CLEAN. The `washed` signal fires when SHINING starts.
4. **Nozzle and hose.** Eased drawing only. The jet itself lands on the raw `_aim`.
5. **Hose loop.** An OGG loop with a code-built band-noise fallback.

Input: the mouse goes through `_gui_input`, gated by a wake latch (`awake()`, WAKE_AFTER 0.4 s after the choosing press is let go). The pad goes through `WashRoom._pad_aim`, which moves the real hidden pointer with `Pad.move_cursor(left stick)` and calls `stand.spray(get_local_mouse_position(), RT or A held)`. `spray()` itself is never gated, so harnesses and probes drive it directly.

The cleanest thing to lift for an uncap minigame (a hot knife revealing honey under wax caps) is the mask alone. That means a RefCounted with no node, no input, no drawing and no sound. It holds `solid`, `amount`, `total`, `left`, the coat Image/ImageTexture, the step cuts, and `wear`, `brush`, `share_clean` and `flush`. It takes the host's RNG and an ink Callable, so the wash's randomness and pixels stay exactly the same.

To leave wash behaviour and the existing tests and probes untouched, WashStand must keep its private names (`_solid`, `_art`, `_region`, `_zoom`, `_grow`, `_fit`, `_stand_top`, `_stand_pad`, `_await_release`, `_asleep`, `_drive_wake`), because tests and probes reach them with `get`/`set`/`call`.

Everything else stays in WashStand: nozzle, hose, stream, runs, flecks, stand drawing. The wake gate and the loop-voice loader are small enough to copy rather than share.

-- PITFALLS
! Packed arrays are value types in GDScript 4. If `_solid`/`_grime` become getters returning a helper's arrays and WashStand code still writes `_solid[i] = ...` or `_grime[i] = ...`, the write goes to a copy and is lost silently. Every write (`_read_solid`, `_roll_coat`, `_wear`, the rinse) must live inside the helper. Per-cell reads through a getter in hot loops (`_wash_at`, `_drive_finish`, `_step_run`) also add a call per cell, so move those loops too.
! Tests and probes reach private names by reflection: test_lake sets `_await_release`/`_asleep` and calls `_drive_wake`/`_fit`/`_stand_top`/`_stand_pad`/`_process`, reads `_zoom`/`_region` and sets `_grow`. shot_pump.gd reads `_art`/`_solid`/`_region`. `Object.set` on a missing name is a silent no-op, so a check then fails with a misleading message. `get` returns null, and shot_pump's `var solid: PackedByteArray = ...get(&'_solid')` would throw in a desktop probe, which looks like a hang. Keep every one of those names on WashStand.
! One RNG drives everything: `_roll` is seeded with `hash(String(piece))`, gives the coat's noise seed, and then feeds the bubble rolls inside `_wear`, the run/fleck spawns and the stars, all interleaved. A helper with its own RNG changes where runs start, and runs wear the coat (RUN_WASH 0.07), so `share_clean` trajectories in tests and probes shift. Pass WashStand's `_roll` instance into the helper.
! `_drive_bubbles` reassigns `_bubbles = kept` (a new array) every frame. A helper that appends bubbles to an array it got by reference drifts away from the host's list after the first frame. The helper must own the list and its ageing, or the host must write back.
! Float precision: `_total` sums float64 amounts, `_grime` stores float32, and `_left` subtracts float32-derived values. So `_left` never lands exactly on 0 and `share_clean()` never equals exactly 1.0 by wearing. The wash sidesteps this with DONE_AT 0.99 and `_left = 0.0` at SHINING. An uncap finish test must not wait for `share_clean() == 1`.
! `_wear` snaps any cell at or below THIN_AT (0.14) to 0, so one call can remove more than `take` and `share_clean` jumps. COAT_LEAST 0.42 > FILM_AT 0.40 means a fresh coat is never in the thin step. Keep the snap and these cuts identical if the wash moves onto a shared helper.
