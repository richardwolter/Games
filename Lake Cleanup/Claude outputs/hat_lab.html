Straw Hat Rig

Four views of the angler, hat drawn by the same maths player.gd uses. Drag a hat where it belongs — or nudge it with the arrow keys — and the constants underneath follow. Offsets are in source pixels, the 32×32 grid the sheet is cut on.


    

      
How to use it

      
    
        
  • Drag a hat, or click a panel and use ← ↑ → ↓ for one source pixel at a time.
    • 
        
  • Side (mirrored) is the same row flipped, the way a leftward walk is drawn. Drag either side panel; the other mirrors it.
    • 
        
  • The head mark is the per-frame point slice_character.gd wrote. The hat is centred on that first, then your offset is added.
    • 
        
  • Brim height and hat width are shared by every view, the way the game holds them.
    • 
        
  • Paste the block back to me and I will fit it to player.gd and the shed's copy of the same hat.
