## Notes

- **Short description: 166 characters** (Python `len()`), well under 300.
- **Register**: 해요체 throughout, as the style guide and the game's own strings; one
  "꾸며 보세요" (polite suggestion) in the short description, which reads naturally as a
  store-page invitation. No "당신"/"여러분": Korean leaves the player unaddressed.
- **Glossary terms** as the game uses them: 그물 (net), 힘 / 크기 / 행운 (Strength / Size /
  Luck), **쌍그물** (Double cast, the shop row's own word), 강아지 (dogs), 집 (house), 씻다
  (wash), 꾸미기/꾸미다 (decorate), 야생 동물 (wildlife), 토네이도, "그물로 토네이도를
  길들여요" mirrors the in-game `TORNADO_FIRST` line. "Objects in the lake" is 물건 (the
  game's word).
- **"Satisfying"** is 속 시원한 / 속이 시원해지는 ("refreshing, gives relief"), the usual
  Korean word for the pleasure of watching dirt wash off. Used three times, as the English
  repeats "satisfying". Check it does not read as "relieved" in a stomach sense; in this
  context it should not.
- **"Cozy, relaxing game"** is 아늑하고 편안한 힐링 게임: 힐링 게임 is the standard Korean
  store and tag term for this genre. A reviewer may prefer dropping 힐링 from the heading.
- **"From grime to beauty"** became 구정물에서 아름다운 호수로 ("from filthy water to a
  beautiful lake"): a literal "때에서 아름다움으로" reads as a calque. 구정물 is plain
  "dirty water", no second meaning.
- **"Not every find is trash"** is 건져 올린 게 다 쓰레기는 아니에요 ("not everything you
  pull up is trash"), fishing-flavoured rather than the literal 발견.
- **"Face the elements"** is 자연의 힘에 맞서요 ("stand up to the power of nature"); "the
  elements" as weather has no tidy Korean equivalent.
- **"Wildlife appreciates your work"** is 야생 동물도 그 노력에 고마워할 거예요 ("will be

## Review

Independent review, 2026-10-10.

Nothing changed. Read for second meanings (더러운 호수, 구정물 read only as dirty water),
gambling (행운, 쌍그물: none), 해요체 with no 당신/여러분, game terms (그물, 힘 / 크기 / 행운 /
쌍그물, 강아지, 집, 씻다, 꾸미다, 길들여요), Steam's term "Steam 도전 과제", PlayStation, and
normal Korean spacing.

Left for the owner:
- "속 시원한" for "satisfying" is the usual word for cleaning content, but it can also carry
  "serves them right"; "보는 맛이 있는" or plain 힐링 wording are alternatives if a native hears that.
- "야생 동물도 그 노력에 고마워할 거예요": "그 노력" (that effort) is a little stiff;
  "야생 동물도 고마워할 거예요" is the simpler option, at the cost of "your work".
