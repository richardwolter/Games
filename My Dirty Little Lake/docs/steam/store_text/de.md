## Notes

- **Short description: 298 characters** (Python `len()`), against the 300 limit. Shortened
  from the English by folding "in this cozy, relaxing game" into "gemütlich und entspannt"
  and dropping "your gear" (now "Geld für Upgrades", the game's own word for the shop).
- **"Satisfying" is never "befriedigend"**: in German that is the school grade 3 (merely
  adequate) and carries a sexual second meaning. Rendered as "wohltuend" (soothing),
  "Wohlfühlen" (feeling good) and "das einfach guttut" (that simply feels good). Check that
  the warmth still lands.
- **"Dirty lake" is "verdreckter See"**, not "schmutzig"/"dreckig", which can also mean
  smutty ("schmutzige Witze"). "Verdreckt" only reads as covered in filth. The heading uses
  "Dreck" (filth), which the game already uses ("den Dreck abzuwaschen").
- **"Clean" is "sauber machen"**, as everywhere in the game; "säubern" avoided (it also
  means "to purge").
- **Glossary terms match the game**: Netz, Stärke, Größe, Glück, Doppelwurf (the concept;
  the shop row is "Doppel"), Objekte, Müll, Haus, Tierwelt, Tornado, bändigen (as in
  `TORNADO_FIRST`), waschen, dekorieren, Upgrades. "Find" is "Fund"; "hidden treasures" is
  "versteckte Schätze" (the game itself says "Deko" for a washed find).
- **Address is "du"**, informal, as the game.
- **"Features"** kept as the heading: it is standard on German Steam pages. "Merkmale" or
  "Highlights" are alternatives.
- **"Trotze den Elementen"** is the natural German idiom for "face the elements". "Vom
  Dreck zur Pracht" is a free rendering of "from grime to beauty" with a little rhythm;
  "Aus Dreck wird Schönheit" is the plainer option.
- **"Interactable wildlife"**: "mit der du interagieren kannst" says what the English says
  and no more.
- "Playstation" corrected to "PlayStation"; "Steam Achievements" is Steam's German term
  "Steam-Errungenschaften". Every tag, `[video ...]` line, URL and `{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}`
  placeholder is unchanged, and the line structure (including the blank lines) matches
